# Recent Screenshots app icon

Edited with the built-in imagegen tool, preserving transparent margins. Final selected asset: app-icon.png.

Prompt:

Simplify this macOS app icon and make it strictly black and white. Preserve only the basic two overlapping screenshot-card shapes and two crop corners on the front card. Pure solid black rounded square tile with plain solid white cards and pure black crop corners. Absolutely flat clean vector-like geometry. Remove all color, all gradients, shading, texture, bevels and shadows. No gray, no teal, no text. Preserve square composition and transparent margin around the tile. This must be an extremely simple monochrome app icon readable at small sizes.
